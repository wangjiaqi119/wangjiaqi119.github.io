Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Momaxuan Game Development Studio handles player information for Beacon Guard and our HTML5 titles distributed on the Meta Instant Games (Facebook and Messenger) platform.

Effective Date: November 24, 2026
Last Updated: November 24, 2026
Applies To: Beacon Guard & Momaxuan Instant Games
Operator: Zhengzhou Hi-Tech Industrial Development Zone Momaxuan Game Development Studio

1Overview & Scope

Zhengzhou Hi-Tech Industrial Development Zone Momaxuan Game Development Studio ("Momaxuan Studio", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting player information in accordance with applicable data protection laws and Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. This policy describes what limited data we access when you play Beacon Guard through Facebook Instant Games or Messenger, how we use it, and how you can request deletion of your data at any time.

2Information We Access via Meta Instant Games

When you launch and play Beacon Guard on Meta Instant Games, we access only the minimum platform-provided information required to deliver core gameplay and social features through the official Meta Instant Games SDK (FBInstant):

  • Basic Player Profile Information: Your app-scoped Facebook Player ID, display name, profile photo URL, and preferred language/locale, used solely to display your identity in-game, render social leaderboards, and customize localized text.
  • Gameplay & Cloud Save Data: In-game progression such as highest wave reached, unlocked upgrades, scores, settings, and preferences stored via Meta's Instant Games cloud storage APIs (FBInstant.player.setDataAsync / getDataAsync) so your progress syncs across devices.
  • Social Context Metadata: Non-sensitive context identifiers (e.g., solo session, Messenger chat thread, or tournament context) provided by Meta when you explicitly choose to play with friends, share results, or join a leaderboard.
  • Technical & Platform Diagnostics: Basic device/platform type (e.g., iOS, Android, Web) and anonymous crash or performance telemetry needed to keep the game stable.

We do not request access to your private messages, friend lists outside Meta's built-in Instant Games context features, email address, phone number, or precise GPS location.

3How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above strictly for legitimate game operations:

  • To initialize game sessions, load and save your gameplay progress, and maintain fair leaderboards.
  • To enable optional social features initiated by you, such as friend score comparisons, Messenger match updates, and tournaments.
  • To monitor service reliability, diagnose technical errors, and prevent cheating or abuse.
  • To serve optional rewarded or interstitial advertisements through Meta Audience Network (where enabled), subject to Meta's own privacy controls and ad preferences.

4Data Sharing, Third Parties & Retention

No Sale of Personal Data: Momaxuan Studio does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Your gameplay and profile data are primarily processed within the Meta Instant Games platform infrastructure in accordance with Meta's Privacy Policy. Where we use trusted infrastructure providers for error diagnostics or optional analytics, those providers are bound by strict confidentiality and data security obligations.

Data Retention: We retain gameplay progress and associated app-scoped identifiers only for as long as you maintain an active connection to the game or as required to comply with legal obligations. Inactive or deleted accounts are purged in accordance with our deletion workflow below.

5User Data Deletion Instructions

You have full control over your data and may revoke game permissions and request deletion of all gameplay data associated with Beacon Guard at any time using either of the following methods:

Method A — Remove via Facebook Account Settings (Self-Service)

  1. Log in to your Facebook account and open Settings & Privacy > Settings.
  2. Navigate to Apps and Websites (or Instant Games in the Security/Permissions menu).
  3. Locate Beacon Guard in your list of active games and click View and Edit or Remove.
  4. Confirm removal and check the option to delete game history and stored activity if prompted. This immediately revokes our access to your Facebook profile information.

Method B — Direct Data Deletion Request to Momaxuan Studio

To request permanent erasure of any cloud save records or support correspondence held in connection with your app-scoped Player ID, send an email to our privacy team with the subject line Data Deletion Request - Beacon Guard and include your Facebook display name or in-game Player ID:

Privacy Contact Email: wangjiaqi119@hotmail.com
We will verify your request and permanently delete all associated player data within 30 calendar days (typically within 7 business days), and send you written confirmation once completed.

6Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for general audiences in compliance with Meta's platform age requirements. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal data to us without appropriate parental consent, please contact us immediately and we will promptly delete such information.

7Policy Updates & Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new gameplay features, platform guidelines, or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
